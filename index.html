Waypoint 01 / 11

Introduction

My Talking Parrot ("the app," "we," "us") is a virtual pet app: talk to the on‑screen parrot and it repeats back what you said in a funny, high‑pitched voice, or interact with it by poking, tickling, feeding it and playing simple mini‑games such as guitar‑strumming and tomato‑tossing. The app has no user accounts and no sign‑in. There is no game server, and the app does not request permission to access the internet at all — everything happens directly on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what the app does with your microphone audio and the small set of preferences it stores on your device. It applies to the app as distributed on Google Play and is effective as of 28 October 2026. By installing or using My Talking Parrot, you agree to the practices described here.

Waypoint 02 / 11

Information we collect

The app asks for no personal information, has no accounts, and cannot connect to the internet — it was not built with any network, analytics, advertising or crash‑reporting services. Everything below stays only on your device.

Microphone audio (temporary, on‑device only)

When you turn on listening, the app uses your device microphone to detect when you speak. The short phrase it hears is held in memory and briefly written to a temporary file in the app's private cache folder so it can be played back to you, pitch‑shifted, through the parrot. That temporary file is overwritten the next time you speak and is never uploaded anywhere — there is no speech‑to‑text, no voice profile, and no cloud processing of any kind.

Parrot colour & sound setting (on‑device)

Which colour parrot you've chosen and whether in‑app sound is switched on. Saved locally so the app remembers your choices the next time you open it. This is never sent anywhere — there's no account for it to be attached to.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts or sign‑in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or calendar. It does not use the camera, and it does not access your photos or files. We do not run any analytics, advertising or crash‑reporting service, and the app has no internet permission — it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else, ever. Your voice and your settings never leave your device.

Waypoint 03 / 11

How we use information

  • The talking‑back effect — using the short recording held in your device's temporary storage to play your voice back to you, pitch‑shifted, through the parrot.
  • Mini‑games & interactions — running the poke, tickle, feed, guitar and tomato‑toss interactions entirely on your device, with no data involved beyond the on‑screen animation.
  • Preferences — remembering your chosen parrot colour and sound setting so the app looks and sounds the way you left it.

That's the complete list — there is no analytics, no advertising and no remote configuration, so there is nothing else for the app to use information for.

Waypoint 04 / 11

Third‑party services & data sharing

We don't sell your information, and we have nothing to sell — the app does not integrate any third‑party analytics, advertising, crash‑reporting or cloud‑storage service, and it does not request permission to use the internet. Nothing is shared with us, with Google, or with anyone else, because nothing is ever sent off your device.

If this ever changes — for example, if a future update adds an online feature — we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent before anything is collected or shared.

Waypoint 05 / 11

App permissions

My Talking Parrot requests only the two permissions it needs for the talking‑back effect. You can review app permissions at any time in Android Settings.

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — lets the app hear what you say so it can play it back to you. The app asks for this permission the first time you try to talk to the parrot. If you say no, the parrot's other interactions (poking, tickling, feeding, mini‑games) still work; only the voice‑repeating effect will be unavailable.
  • Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS) — lets the app adjust audio routing/volume while recording and playing back the pitch‑shifted voice effect. This is a standard, non‑sensitive permission that Android grants automatically.

The app does not request internet access, camera, contacts, calendar, SMS, call log, storage/files, location, or any other permission.

Waypoint 06 / 11

Ads & purchases

My Talking Parrot shows no ads and offers no in‑app purchases. There is no advertising SDK and no billing integration of any kind in the app, so no advertising data is collected and no money ever changes hands.

If we add advertising or purchases in a future update, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent.

Waypoint 07 / 11

Storage, security & retention

Your parrot colour and sound setting are stored in a small local preferences file in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The short voice clip used for the talking‑back effect is written to the app's temporary cache folder and overwritten each time you speak. There is no cloud account and no game database — nothing is backed up to us because we have nowhere to receive it.

Device backup. We do not run any backup service. Android's own Auto Backup may include the app's small local preferences file in your Google account backup if backup is switched on in your device settings; that is managed by Android and Google, not by us, and never includes the temporary voice clip.

Retention. Your settings stay on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. The temporary voice clip is overwritten the next time you use the talking‑back feature and is cleared when the app's cache is cleared, when the app is closed by the system, or on uninstall.

Waypoint 08 / 11

Your rights & choices

Wherever you live, the app gives you direct control over the little it stores. Depending on your region (for example, GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may also have statutory rights to access or delete personal data held about you.

Delete your local data with Android Settings → Apps → My Talking Parrot → Storage → Clear storage. This erases your parrot colour, sound setting and any cached voice clip from the device.

Revoke the microphone permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → My Talking Parrot → Permissions. The parrot's poking, tickling, feeding and mini‑games keep working without it.

Change your parrot's colour and the sound setting at any time from within the app.

Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device, including the preferences file and any cached voice clip.

Because the app has no server, no account and no internet connection, the controls above are the only data it holds anywhere — there is nothing further for us to access, export or delete on your behalf.

Waypoint 09 / 11

Children's privacy

My Talking Parrot is a general‑audience entertainment app, is not directed at children under 13, and is not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — and in fact the app does not collect personal information from anyone, since it has no accounts, no internet permission and no server to send data to. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will take appropriate action.
